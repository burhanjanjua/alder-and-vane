Alder & Vane

Engineering rigor. Design sensitivity.

Founded in Scottsdale in 2016 by systems engineer Sam Alder and interior architecture specialist Ruth Vane. Sam brings 20 years in AV; Ruth leads client design.

Their 14-person multidisciplinary team includes two project managers, a system designer, six technicians, a programmer, service coordinator and office manager.

Approximately four out of five projects are luxury new homes and major remodels. Commercial work includes private offices, boardrooms, a members’ club and two medical practices.

Licensed Arizona contractor.
